# Metadusk ecosystem and corporate relationship

## Parent identity

**Metadusk Inc.** is the intended parent-company identity and technology ecosystem for a portfolio of technology businesses and digital products.

Metadusk's role is to build, own, coordinate and grow distinct ventures across technology-led markets. The ecosystem is designed to accommodate additional companies and products over time; it is not limited to the ventures listed here.

**Important legal-status note:** The founder has stated that Metadusk has not yet been incorporated and has not selected its incorporation jurisdiction. Accordingly, this document records the intended corporate and brand structure, not proof of incorporation, legal ownership, subsidiary status, registered address, or completed IP assignments. Do not describe any venture as a legally incorporated subsidiary unless that status is separately confirmed.

### PharmaCore

### TradeMate

TradeMate is a distinct global contract-first business platform in the Metadusk ecosystem. Its public endorsement is:

**TradeMate by Metadusk**

TradeMate retains its own product name, product identity and tagline:

**Trade Smart. Trade Direct.**

TradeMate is not to be described as a conventional shopping marketplace, product checkout platform, cryptocurrency exchange, or spot/futures crypto trading platform. Its central workflow connects counterparties to discover opportunities, communicate, negotiate terms, create and manage business contracts, and confirm fulfilment.

TradeMate's current product scope, technical implementation, release status and roadmap are documented in the main README, architecture document and roadmap. P2P crypto and wallet capabilities remain a separate later-stage workstream and must not be conflated with the core contract workflow.

## Brand architecture rules

- Metadusk is the parent-company and ecosystem identity.
- Deukleinz Technologies and PharmaCore retain their own distinct venture identities.
- TradeMate retains its independent product identity and uses the approved endorsement **TradeMate by Metadusk**.
- Do not use the superseded Nexora parent identity in current branding or documentation.
- Do not use DEU Kleinz Technologies as the parent-company name. Deukleinz Technologies remains a separate venture in the ecosystem.
- Do not imply that the ventures are separately incorporated subsidiaries until their legal status is verified.
- Do not invent company registration numbers, jurisdictions, addresses, directors, ownership percentages, official domains, email addresses, or trademark registrations.
- Additional ventures may be added as the ecosystem grows.

## Intellectual property and trademark direction

The founder intends Metadusk to be the owner of the parent brand and its trademark strategy, while individual ventures and products retain their distinct brands. The founder has conducted preliminary name searches and is considering staged protection across priority jurisdictions.

This is a statement of intended direction, not legal clearance or a claim that METADUSK is registered or conclusively available worldwide. Before filing or transferring any mark, confirm the applicant entity's legal existence, ownership chain, relevant classes, jurisdictional clearance and assignment/licensing documentation with qualified trademark counsel.
