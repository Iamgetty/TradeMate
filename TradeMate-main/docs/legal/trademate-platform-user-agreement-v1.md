# TradeMate Platform User Agreement

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 28 November 2026  
**Platform:** TradeMate

## Welcome to TradeMate

TradeMate exists to make serious business and marketplace relationships clearer, more transparent and more trustworthy. It brings people, businesses and organizations together to discover opportunities, communicate, negotiate and build relationships with greater confidence.

By selecting **I agree and continue**, you confirm that you have read and accept this Agreement. Your acceptance is recorded against your account, together with the version of this Agreement and the time of acceptance.

## 1. Your account and information

You agree to provide information that is accurate, current and not misleading. Keep your sign-in credentials private, use your account responsibly and tell us promptly if you believe someone else has accessed it.

You are responsible for activity carried out through your account, except where applicable law says otherwise. Do not impersonate another person, create deceptive accounts or use another person's identity or documents without proper authority.

## 2. Honest and responsible use

Use TradeMate for lawful, genuine business and marketplace activity. Do not use the platform to deceive, defraud, harass, threaten, manipulate, facilitate unlawful activity or misrepresent a person, organization, product, service, opportunity or transaction.

Do not upload documents that are false, altered to mislead, stolen, unlawfully obtained or unrelated to the activity for which they are requested. You must have the right and authority to share information and documents you submit.

## 3. Identity, organizations and verification

TradeMate may ask you or an organization you represent to complete identity, business or other verification checks. You agree to provide truthful information and cooperate with reasonable verification requests.

Verification is a risk and identity-checking process. A verified profile does not mean TradeMate guarantees a person's honesty, financial position, professional ability, ownership claims or future conduct. It is not a substitute for your own due diligence.

Do not claim to represent an organization unless you are authorized to do so. Organization access and document visibility may depend on your current role and permissions.

## 4. Marketplace activity, negotiations and contracts

You are responsible for reviewing the details of an opportunity, offer, negotiation or transaction before you act on it. Make sure you understand the parties, scope, price, currency, timing, obligations, delivery terms and any conditions that matter to you.

TradeMate provides tools that help parties discover one another, communicate and manage commercial workflows. Unless a specific service clearly says otherwise, TradeMate is not a party to the agreement between users and does not guarantee that another user will perform, pay, deliver, remain available or meet a stated qualification.

A message, draft, uploaded file, proposal, expression of interest or in-platform status must not be treated as a completed or legally signed contract unless the relevant parties have completed the applicable agreement and signature process. Do not describe a document as signed, approved, official or complete when it is not.

You remain responsible for obtaining independent legal, financial, tax, regulatory or professional advice where appropriate.

## 5. Documents, privacy and permissions

Documents and information you submit may be stored and made available to you through your TradeMate document area. Access is controlled according to the document's purpose and the permissions associated with your account, organization or transaction.

Do not attempt to access, download, disclose, copy or use another person's or organization's private information without permission. You authorize TradeMate to process information and documents as reasonably necessary to provide, secure, support and improve the platform, comply with legal obligations and address abuse or disputes, subject to applicable privacy requirements and TradeMate's privacy notice.

Only share personal information about other people when you have a lawful basis and the authority to do so.

## 6. Platform integrity and security

Do not probe, disrupt, overload, reverse engineer or bypass platform security or access controls. Do not introduce malicious code, scrape private information, interfere with another user's access or misuse platform features.

TradeMate may investigate suspicious activity, restrict access, preserve relevant records or take other proportionate steps where reasonably necessary to protect users, the platform or the integrity of a transaction, or to comply with law.

## 7. Availability and changes

We work to keep TradeMate reliable and secure, but uninterrupted availability cannot be guaranteed. Features may change, be suspended or be withdrawn as the platform develops or where maintenance, security, legal or operational needs require it.

We may update this Agreement. When a material update requires renewed acceptance, we will present the updated version and record your decision. An earlier acceptance does not count as acceptance of a later version.

## 8. Suspension and account closure

We may limit or suspend access where we reasonably believe an account is compromised, these rules have been breached, activity presents a risk to users or the platform, or we are required to do so by law. Where appropriate, we will provide information about the action and any available review process.

You may stop using TradeMate and request account closure through the available account process. Certain records may need to be retained for security, audit, dispute-resolution or legal reasons.

## 9. No blanket guarantee

TradeMate provides tools and information to support better business interactions. We do not promise that every user, listing, opportunity, document, statement or transaction is accurate, suitable or risk-free. You should verify important claims and make decisions using your own judgment.

Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits a right or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable law.

## 10. Your acceptance

By selecting **I agree and continue**, you confirm that:

- You have read and understood this Agreement.
- The information you provide and the documents you submit will be truthful and shared with proper authority.
- You will use TradeMate lawfully, respectfully and in good faith.
