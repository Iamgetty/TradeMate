# TradeMate marketplace taxonomy

**Status:** Approved product direction  
**Marketplace model:** Hybrid — one unified global discovery marketplace with dedicated specialist markets inside each division.

TradeMate is a contract-first opportunity and deal-making platform. The taxonomy organises what counterparties can discover; transaction type defines what they want to do. The same deal-room foundations can support different industries, but each division requires its own listing fields, permissions, verification and legal workflow.

## 1. Sports

- **Sports:** Football, basketball, American football, baseball, rugby, cricket, tennis, ice hockey, volleyball, athletics, boxing, MMA, golf, swimming, motorsport, esports and other sports.
- **Talent and recruitment:** Athlete recruitment, player transfers where permitted, loans where permitted, free-agent signings, coaches and technical staff.
- **Representation:** Sports agents, athlete management and coaching representation.
- **Commercial:** Sponsorships, endorsements, team and club partnerships, broadcasting rights, event hosting and sports-facility agreements.

Transfers, trades, free agency and contract rules vary by sport and league. TradeMate must not impose football transfer mechanics on every sport. Athletes remain consenting parties, not property.

## 2. Entertainment & Media

- **Talent and representation:** Artists, musicians, actors, directors, producers, presenters, talent agencies and creator representation.
- **Music:** Record labels, recording agreements, music publishing, rights and royalties, distribution, bookings, studios and production.
- **Film and television:** Film and TV production, casting and crew, distribution, licensing and broadcasting rights.
- **Art and design:** Visual artists, galleries, representation, artwork sales, commissions, exhibitions and design licensing.
- **Live events:** Concerts, festivals, touring, event production and venue partnerships.
- **Media:** Publishing, podcasts, advertising, content licensing and syndication.

People are never listed as property. Agreements concern voluntary employment, representation, licensing, bookings, services and rights.

## Transaction types across the marketplace

A listing's division/category describes what the opportunity concerns. Its transaction type describes what the parties want to do.

- Available / for sale.
- Seeking buyer.
- Acquisition.
- Transfer or loan, where the relevant industry permits it.
- Recruitment or appointment.
- Representation.
- Lease or rental.
- Licensing.
- Investment or financing request.
- Partnership or joint venture.
- Service contract.
- Supply agreement.
- Tender / request for proposal.
- Concession or operating agreement.

Not every transaction type applies to every division. Each specialist market must expose only valid options.

## Shared TradeMate infrastructure

1. Unified global search and discovery, with division-specific filters.
2. Specialist listings and structured opportunity requests.
3. Organisation, individual and authority verification appropriate to the division.
4. Offers, proposals, counteroffers and private negotiation.
5. Deal rooms for documents and due diligence.
6. Contract drafting, review, acceptance and fulfilment tracking.
7. Appropriate payment-provider integrations and transaction records, subject to legal and provider approval.
8. Disputes, audit trails, reputation and reporting.

## Product boundaries and release status

- P2P crypto remains a separate workflow and is not a synonym for the contract marketplace.
- Crypto and Forex market data remain market-information surfaces, not crypto/forex trading services.
- Conventional product-cart checkout is not part of this approved taxonomy.
- The taxonomy is a product architecture, not a statement that all specialist workflows are already implemented. The released persistent contract workflow remains limited to the currently supported Services and B2B paths until each additional division is integrated and tested.
- Verification, contracts, payments, escrow, custody, investment, healthcare, property, public procurement and other regulated workflows require jurisdiction- and activity-specific legal/compliance review.
